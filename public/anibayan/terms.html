1. About AniBayan

AniBayan is an online marketplace that helps buyers discover harvests offered by farmer organizations in Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro. Each organization is responsible for its own listings, the accuracy of its product information, and preparing and fulfilling its orders. AniBayan provides the marketplace, order tools, payment-status tools, and a channel for reporting problems.

  • Give accurate account and contact information, keep your password private, and tell AniBayan if you believe someone else has accessed your account.
  • Use only accounts you are authorized to use. Do not impersonate another person, misrepresent a farmer organization, or use the service for unlawful activity.
  • Organization representatives must provide truthful application details and documents and keep their listings, availability, harvest information, prices, and contact details current.

3. Listings, prices, and orders

Listings describe the harvest, unit, price, and availability provided by the organization. Harvests are seasonal and stock can change. The order total and any applicable charges shown at checkout are the amount to review before placing the order. AniBayan creates a separate order for each organization in a buyer’s cart.

An order is a request to buy from the organization shown on that order. The organization prepares and fulfills it and may contact the buyer using the delivery details supplied at checkout. Buyers should provide a complete address and working mobile number. Delivery arrangements are coordinated with the organization; any available delivery terms or charges will be shown before the buyer confirms the order.

4. Payment and payment status

  • Available methods may include cash on delivery, GCash or QR Ph, and card payments, depending on what checkout offers at the time.
  • For online payments, AniBayan uses PayMongo. An order is not marked paid until AniBayan receives or verifies a successful payment status. If a payment is pending, check its status before trying to pay again.
  • Card information is handled by PayMongo and is not stored by AniBayan.
  • If checkout identifies a payment as a test or simulation, it is for testing and does not charge real money. Follow the payment screen shown for the specific transaction.
