Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-27 · Last updated November 27, 2026

This notice explains how AniBayan uses personal information when you browse, create an account, apply as a farmer organization, place an order, or contact support.

1. Who is responsible

AniBayan is the personal information controller for information collected through the AniBayan marketplace. The AniBayan team can be contacted through the support chat widget available on the website. Use that chat to ask a privacy question, request access or correction, or raise a concern; ask for the AniBayan privacy contact if your request needs to be referred.

When an order is sent to a farmer organization, that organization also handles the buyer information it receives to prepare and fulfill the order. Ask AniBayan support to route a privacy request about that organization’s use of the information.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number, account role, password credential, preferences, and account/security activity. Passwords are stored as protected password hashes.
  • Buyer and order details: delivery address, optional map pin, phone number, items and quantities, order notes, payment method, payment reference and status, and refund or complaint details.
  • Organization applications: organization and representative details, barangay, crops, contact details, and documents submitted for review, such as registration papers, a barangay endorsement, or a government-issued representative ID. These documents may contain sensitive personal information.
  • Marketplace content: product listings, member-farmer details, reviews, reports, and images or files that users choose to submit.
  • Support and technical information: messages you send through the support chat and basic connection, session, and security information needed to operate the website.

3. Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts; review organization applications; display listings and profiles; place, prepare, deliver, and track orders; verify payments and handle refunds; answer support requests; moderate reviews and reports; maintain service security; and comply with legal obligations. We process information for these purposes using the applicable legal basis, such as providing the service you request, meeting a legal obligation, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or consent where required.

Organization applicants are asked for a separate, explicit consent before submitting representative identity and organization documents. We use those files only to review and administer the application and organization account.

4. Who receives information

  • The farmer organization fulfilling an order receives the buyer’s name, order details, delivery address, phone number, and any delivery note needed to prepare and deliver that order.
  • PayMongo processes online payment and billing information so it can create and verify a payment. AniBayan receives payment references and status. AniBayan does not store full card numbers or security codes.
  • Authorized AniBayan administrators can review account, application, order, complaint, and payment information when needed to operate the service, review applications, resolve disputes, and protect users.
  • Service providers help operate the website: Render hosts the application, Supabase provides its database, PayMongo processes payments, and Tawk.to provides support chat. The database is currently configured in Supabase’s EU West region. These providers may process information in countries outside the Philippines under their own service arrangements.
  • Reviews, listings, and organization profiles may be visible to other visitors. Buyer profile information is shown according to the privacy settings in the account.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or a valid request from a competent authority, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Payments, chat, and cookies

Payment details are handled by PayMongo according to the payment method selected. For support chat, information you send is processed by Tawk.to as well as AniBayan so the team can respond. The site uses essential session and security cookies to keep sign-in and requests working; the embedded support chat may use its own cookies or similar technologies.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and profile information while an account is active. Order, payment, refund, application, complaint, and security records may be kept for as long as needed to complete the transaction, administer the service, resolve disputes, meet legal or accounting duties, and establish or defend legal claims. We then delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable law and technical backup cycles.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update some profile, address, and privacy settings in your account. Under the Data Privacy Act, you may also ask whether we process your personal information, request access or correction, object or request blocking/deletion where the law allows, ask about data portability, or lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Some records may need to be retained to complete an order or comply with law.

To make a request, contact AniBayan using the website support chat and include the email address on your account and what you need help with. We may ask for information to verify that you control the account before acting on a request.

8. Keeping information safe

AniBayan uses access controls and technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and contact support promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the service or data practices change. The current version and its date will be posted here. If a change needs renewed acknowledgment, we will show the updated notice before the relevant account or order action.

Privacy contact
Use the support chat widget on the AniBayan website and ask for the AniBayan privacy contact. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission about your data-subject rights.
